The House at the end of the road

Some Doors should stay shut.

Chapter 1 : The Strange house

It was 🎃Halloween🎃 Night and the streets were preety much empty. Alex, who was seventeen, was walking home from his friend's place with his hands shoved in his jacket pockets. A cold wind kept pushing through the trees. He'd walked this road a hundered times, but tonight it felt off, and he could not say why.

At the ery end of the road there was an old house. The windows were dark and the front door hung half open. Nobody in the town ever talked about it, like they'd all agreed to forget it was there. Alex never paid it any attentation either, until he saw a little orange light glowing in an upstairs window.

Chapter 2 : The Wishper

He knew he should keep walking. Instead he opened the gate and went in. the grass was up to his knees and the steps creaked under him. When he pushed the door, it swung open with a long, ugly groan.

It was freezing inside. Dust covered everything, and there was a big old clock stuck at midnight. Alex pulled out his phone and turned on the flashlight.Thats when he heard a whisper coming from up stairs.

"You are late!!!"

Alex froze. Nobody knew he was here. hw wasnot even sure why he was here. he called out, but nobody answered. then the clock start ticking, even though the hands still said twelve

Chapter 3 : The Photograph

On a dusty table he found a photo in a wooden frame. It showed a family standing in front of exact house. The faces were faded and hard to see, but something about the boy at the end of the row made Alex Step back.

It was him. Same face, same messy hair, even the same jacket he had on right now.

Upstairs a door slammed. Alex ran for the exit, but the frontdooor wouldnot open, no matter how hard he pulled. Above him, slow footstep started across the floor, getting closer to the stairs.

The End

Somehow Alex got the door open and ran all the way home without looking back. The next morning he told himself it was just a bad dream. He almost believed it

Then he put his hand in his jacket pocket and found the photo. Someone had written on the back....

"See you in Next 🎃Halloween🎃 Alex"
